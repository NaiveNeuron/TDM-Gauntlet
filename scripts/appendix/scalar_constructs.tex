\paragraph{Task.} Scalar measurement of three constructs in English U.S. political text: immigration fear in survey
answers (Imm.), ad negativity (Ad) and grandstanding in hearings (Grand.). Gold item scores are Bradley--Terry
(BT) fits to crowd pairwise comparisons. The paper's task-specific model is a DeBERTa-v3-large reward model
(Table~3). \emph{Pointwise}: \jev{} rates one text on the paper's scale. \emph{Pairwise}: \jev{} picks the text
with more of the construct, and a BT fit to its choices gives the item scores.

The test set is fold~0 of the released graph splits, which is what Table~2 uses. Pairwise: all fold-0 test
pairs, $N=$ <<s:scalar_constructs/immigration_fear/pairwise:n|int>> /

\paragraph{Prompt and metric.} Both modes use the paper's zero-shot prompts (App.~E) verbatim, minus the
output-format line. \emph{Pointwise}: one \textsc{Score} request per text, and the prediction is the
probability-weighted mean level (Eq.~1). \emph{Pairwise}: a \textsc{Choice} over \{1, 2\} in the paper's four
debiasing presentations (App.~A.2), averaged. The state is the release's pair string, byte-identical.
