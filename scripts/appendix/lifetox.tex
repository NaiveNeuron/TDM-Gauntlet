\paragraph{Task.} English advice question--answer pairs from r/LifeProTips and r/UnethicalLifeProTips.
Labels are binary, \textsc{safe}/\textsc{unsafe}. The replaced predictor is the paper's fine-tuned safety classifier
(RoBERTa-LifeTox). \jev{} sees the question and one answer and chooses the released label.

\paragraph{Data and freeze.} Hugging Face \texttt{<<m:lifetox:source.hf_dataset>>} at revision
<<m:lifetox:source.hf_revision|commit>>. Licence: MIT, so \texttt{scripts/\allowbreak{}prepare\_lifetox.py} freezes the test
inputs as JSONL (SHA-256 <<m:lifetox:frozen.sha256|sha>>).

\paragraph{Test set.} $N=$ <<s:lifetox:n|int>> (<<m:lifetox:frozen.label_counts|counts>>), the complete released
test file, matching the paper's App.~A.2. The release's total differs from the paper's only in unsafe
\emph{training} rows.

\paragraph{English screen.} Language identification on every query and response plus a non-ASCII check.
Every flag was inspected and all were false positives (short strings, URLs, emoticons).
<<m:lifetox:english_verification.rows_dropped|int>> rows dropped.

\paragraph{Prompt.} One \textsc{choice} question per item over \{<<q:lifetox:question.criteria|keys>>\}. State =
\{question, answer\}. The instruction is the paper's own zero-shot LLM-baseline prompt (App.~B.2) and the
label criteria quote its definitions (\S1, Fig.~1). Prediction: argmax, exact ties to \textsc{safe}.
<<s:lifetox:n_truncated|int>> inputs truncated.

\paragraph{Metric.} Macro-F1 over the two classes (paper Table~2). ROC-AUC uses $P(\textsc{unsafe})$.
