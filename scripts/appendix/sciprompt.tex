\paragraph{Task.} Single-label topic classification of English scientific abstracts over four fixed inventories:
arXiv categories (<<q:sciprompt_arxiv:question.criteria|len>> classes), Semantic Scholar fields (S2ORC, S2 in tables,
<<q:sciprompt_s2orc:question.criteria|len>>), SDPRA-2021 CS domains (<<q:sciprompt_sdpra:question.criteria|len>>)
and Emerging NLP topics (ENLP, <<q:sciprompt_emerging_nlp:question.criteria|len>>). Replaced predictor: the paper's
knowledge-augmented prompt-tuned SciBERT (SciPrompt). \jev{} chooses each abstract's topic.

\paragraph{Data and freeze.} The authors' data archive linked from \texttt{<<m:sciprompt:source.code_repo>>}
(<<m:sciprompt:source.code_commit|commit>>, zip SHA-256 <<m:sciprompt:source.raw_zip_sha256|sha>>), using the test
files the paper's scripts read. \texttt{scripts/prepare\_sciprompt.py} freezes SDPRA (CC BY 4.0) and Emerging NLP
(Apache-2.0) as JSONL. ArXiv and S2ORC state no licence, so for them it stores only row-SHA-256 manifests and
rebuilds the text.

\paragraph{Test sets and English screen.} Released test sets are balanced per class and match paper Table~5. For
SDPRA we apply the paper's own short-abstract filter (recomputed with its code:
<<s:sciprompt/sdpra:n_excluded_short|int>> rows), which reproduces its evaluated N. A multi-signal screen (langid,
function words, scripts and foreign keyword headings, with all candidates read) excluded abstracts with non-English
sentences or keyword lines: arXiv <<s:sciprompt/arxiv:n_excluded_non_english|int>>, S2ORC
<<s:sciprompt/s2orc:n_excluded_non_english|int>>, SDPRA <<s:sciprompt/sdpra:n_excluded_non_english|int>>, Emerging NLP
<<s:sciprompt/emerging_nlp:n_excluded_non_english|int>>.

\paragraph{Prompt and metric.} One \textsc{choice} per abstract over the full inventory. State = \{abstract\}. The
instruction is the paper's zero-shot LLM instruction (App.~G) with its field-word list replaced by typed options:
arXiv's official category names and descriptions (arXiv, SDPRA), field names only (S2ORC), and Emerging NLP class
names with their Taxonomy4CL position. Argmax. Ties to the lower released label id. Truncated:
<<s:sciprompt/arxiv:n_truncated|int>>. Accuracy (Tables~1--2, Fig.~3). Macro-F1 secondary.

\paragraph{Deviations and status.} Complete. Emerging NLP paper values are read from the Fig.~3 bar chart. Table~1 of
the main paper compares with the best trained setting per dataset. The matching zero-shot comparison is paper
Table~2 and Fig.~3. \jev{} exceeds every configuration the paper reports on arXiv, SDPRA and Emerging NLP (on S2ORC
only the 50-shot and fully fine-tuned models are higher). The arXiv margin is the largest and is recorded as
anomalous. We verified it (balanced gold, predictions spread over all classes, accuracy recomputed), and two factors
likely contribute: \jev{}'s options include arXiv's official descriptions, whereas the paper's LLM baselines saw only
field words (some of which name the wrong category), and arXiv abstracts are a probable pretraining exposure (a
risk that also applies to the arXiv-sourced Emerging NLP and SDPRA abstracts).
