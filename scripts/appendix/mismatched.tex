\paragraph{Task.} Scientific NLI on English sentence pairs from Psychology, Engineering and Public Health articles:
\textsc{entailment}, \textsc{reasoning}, \textsc{contrasting} or \textsc{neutral} (the first three derived from a
deleted linking phrase at the start of Sentence2). Replaced predictor: SciBERT fine-tuned on out-of-domain MSciNLI+.
\jev{} chooses the relation.

\paragraph{Data and test set.} \texttt{test.tsv} from the Drive archive linked by the authors' repository
(<<m:mismatched:source.code_repo>>), CC BY-SA 4.0, frozen as JSONL by \texttt{scripts/\allowbreak{}prepare\_mismatched.py} (SHA-256 <<m:mismatched:frozen.sha256|sha>>).
$N=$ <<s:mismatched:n|int>>, the paper's full test set, balanced over domains and classes. English screen (langid,
non-ASCII census, review of flags): <<m:mismatched:english_verification.rows_dropped|int>> rows dropped.

\paragraph{Prompt.} One \textsc{choice} per pair. State = \{Sentence1, Sentence2\}. Question and option texts are the
authors' zero-shot LLM prompt verbatim (from their code). Each option adds its class name and defining linking
phrases (Table~11), and the instruction notes that a leading linking phrase was deleted. No exemplars. Argmax.
<<s:mismatched:n_truncated|int>> truncated.

\paragraph{Metric.} Macro-F1 over the four classes pooled over all domains, as in the authors' code (not the mean
of domain scores).
