\paragraph{Task.} English utterances from six public intent benchmarks (ACID, ATIS, BANKING77 (B77), CLINC150 (C150),
HWU64, MTOD). The paper feeds a fine-tuned BERT's softmax into split conformal prediction: the nonconformity score is
$1-p_{\text{true}}$ on a calibration split, $\hat q$ is its MAPIE quantile at the paper's per-dataset $1-\alpha$, and
the set holds every intent with $p\geq 1-\hat q$. The system answers directly (set size 1), asks a clarification
question, or rejects the input (size $>\mathit{th}=$ <<s:cicc/mtod:th|int>>). \jev{} replaces only BERT: one
\textsc{<<q:cicc_c150:_primitive|upper>>} over the full intent inventory per calibration and test utterance, with
the conformal procedure unchanged. Excluded: the unreleased Dutch industry set, the commercial Dialogflow CX setting
and the generated clarification wording.

\paragraph{Data and freeze.} \url{https://github.com/florisdenhengst/cicc} at <<m:cicc:source.code_commit|commit>>.
Its released calibration/test prediction files are the paper's exact splits and are used directly. The corpora's
licences are mixed or missing (ACID has none, ATIS is LDC-derived), so \texttt{data/frozen/cicc/} holds only row
coordinates, gold labels and per-row SHA-256s. \texttt{scripts/prepare\_cicc.py} rebuilds the text from the pinned
clone and verifies every hash.

\paragraph{Test set and screen.} All released calibration and test rows are used (test $N$ below). C150 is the
in-scope set: the release's \texttt{oos} rows are dropped, as in the authors' notebook. The HWU64 release has
<<m:cicc:splits.hwu64.intents|int>> intents. All <<m:cicc:english_verification.flags|int>> language-screen flags
were checked and are English. No row was dropped.

\paragraph{Prompt.} State = \{utterance\}. The options are the released intent names with underscores as spaces
(MTOD adds the intent's domain). No definitions exist in any release. The largest inventory (ACID,
<<m:cicc:splits.acid.intents|int>> options) is within \jev{}'s option limit, so no decomposition is needed.
Probabilities are renormalised to sum to one, and omitted intents get 0. Truncated inputs:
<<s:cicc/acid:n_truncated|int>>.

\paragraph{Metric.} The primary metric is Single: the share of test inputs answered without clarification at the
paper's $1-\alpha$ (Table~2, p.~2418). Coverage over non-rejected inputs, mean set size and top-1 accuracy are
secondary. Our scorer, a port of the authors' \texttt{eval.py} and MAPIE~0.6.5, reproduces their
\texttt{results.csv} from the released BERT probabilities.
