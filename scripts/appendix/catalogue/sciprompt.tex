@task
Given the abstract of a scientific paper, choose its research field or topic from a fixed list. Four datasets range
from broad disciplines to fine-grained NLP topics.
@output
One label per abstract, from <<q:sciprompt_arxiv:question.criteria|len>> arXiv categories,
<<q:sciprompt_s2orc:question.criteria|len>> S2ORC fields, <<q:sciprompt_sdpra:question.criteria|len>>
SDPRA computer-science domains or <<q:sciprompt_emerging_nlp:question.criteria|len>> Emerging-NLP topics, depending on the dataset.
@example frozen SDPRA test set, CC BY 4.0, item \texttt{<<jl:data/frozen/sciprompt/sdpra.jsonl:25.id>>}
Abstract & <<jl:data/frozen/sciprompt/sdpra.jsonl:25.abstract|c220>>
Gold & <<jl:data/frozen/sciprompt/sdpra.jsonl:25.gold>>
@sets
sciprompt/arxiv = arXiv | abstracts
sciprompt/s2orc = S2ORC | abstracts
sciprompt/sdpra = SDPRA | abstracts
sciprompt/emerging_nlp = Emerging-NLP | abstracts
