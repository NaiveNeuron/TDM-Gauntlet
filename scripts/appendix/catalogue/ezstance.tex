@task
Given a tweet and a target (a short noun phrase or a full claim sentence), decide whether the tweet's author is in
favour of, against, or neutral toward the target. Targets and, in Subtask B, whole topic domains are unseen in
training.
@output
One label from \{<<q:ezstance:noun_phrase.criteria|keys>>\} (released as <<q:ezstance:_label_map.Favor>>/<<q:ezstance:_label_map.Against>>/<<q:ezstance:_label_map.Neutral>>).
