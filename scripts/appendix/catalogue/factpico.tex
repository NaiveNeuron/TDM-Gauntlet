@task
Rate how faithfully a plain-language summary of a clinical-trial abstract reports the trial's population,
intervention, comparator, outcome and results. Medical experts gave the gold ratings.
@output
Ordinal scores: <<q:factpico:pico.population.criteria|len>> levels per PICO element (incl.\ N/A),
<<q:factpico:evidence_inference.criteria|len>> levels per result span. The overall score is their mean.
